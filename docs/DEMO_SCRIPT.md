# Two-minute prospect demo

Use this when you screen-record the CRM for a roofing company. The clock times assume the sample leads are already loaded. Those people are fictional: `example.com` emails, `555-010` phone numbers, and streets such as Demo Lane. Say that out loud. Do not call them customers.

Sign in at `/admin/login` with the staff account for this install before you start the clock. Leave the dashboard open.

## 0:00 — Dashboard

Click **Dashboard** if you are not already there.

Say: “This is the morning view. New leads today, hot leads still open, how many are in the pipeline, how many were marked won this month, and how many follow-ups are overdue. The names in here are sample leads, not real homeowners.”

Point at the five numbers. Do not read a customer count. There isn’t one.

## 0:20 — Lead list

Click **New leads today**, or **View all leads**.

Say: “Every website request is one row: name, priority, phone, email, city, what they asked for, how soon, and the status.”

Click the search box, type `Casey`, and open **Casey Demo**.

## 0:40 — Lead detail

Say: “Casey is a sample emergency from this morning. Here is the phone, the email, the address, the roof, and the note. If they had uploaded photos, they would be on this page. If they asked for a text and checked the consent box, the exact wording, the time, and the IP address are here too.”

Point at **AI scoring** only long enough to say: “The priority is a suggestion. You can re-run it. You still decide what to do.”

Open the status menu, choose **Contacted**, and click **Save status**. Then set it back to **New** if you want the sample left as you found it.

## 1:05 — Tasks

On the same lead, point at **Follow-up tasks**.

Say: “A new request gets a task so it does not sit until Monday. I can add one, mark it done, or snooze it an hour or a day.”

Click **Tasks** in the top bar.

Say: “Overdue, today, upcoming, and completed. The badge on Tasks is the overdue count.”

## 1:25 — New lead

Click **New lead**.

Say: “A call that never touched the website gets entered here. Same fields as the homeowner form: name, phone, email, address, roof, and what they need. I won’t save a throwaway during this recording.”

Show the empty form. Do not submit.

## 1:40 — CSV

Click **Import**.

Say: “If the leads already live in a spreadsheet, import the CSV. Bad rows are reported. They are not silently dropped into the list.”

Click **Export**.

Say: “Export downloads the current leads as a CSV. That file is the copy you keep.”

Let the download start. You do not need to open it.

## 1:55 — Close

Say: “Homeowners use the inspection form. The office works the list here. We are starting free pilots. This recording is the product, not a promise of a customer list we do not have.”

Stop the recording.
